AO-103 · CONTROLS

Reaction-wheel inverted pendulum

Modeled and stabilized “The Nacho Supreme,” a reaction-wheel inverted pendulum, in MATLAB/Simulink. Identified the motor, pendulum, and IMU dynamics — the IMU proved to be a dynamic filter, not a unity gain — then tuned a PID controller to a 0.41 s settling time with 7.2% overshoot.
